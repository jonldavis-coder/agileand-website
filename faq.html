FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how we work. Don't see your question? Just ask.

What does Agile& do?

Agile& provides agile training, team and leadership coaching, agile assessments, and transformation advisory. We help organizations adopt agile ways of working in a practical way that fits their context.

How do you pronounce Agile&?

“Agile and.” The ampersand reflects our belief that agility matters when it's paired with an outcome: agile and better products, agile and happier customers, agile and sustainable teams.

Do you use a specific agile framework?

We draw on widely used practices such as Scrum, Kanban, and lean thinking, and adapt them to your situation. We don't believe in installing a framework for its own sake.

Who is your training for?

Teams new to agile, Scrum Masters and coaches, product owners and managers, and leaders. Workshops are delivered privately for your organization and tailored to your context.

Can training be delivered online?

Yes. Workshops can be delivered live online or on-site, and formats range from a half day to a series of shorter sessions.

Do your courses lead to certification?

Our workshops focus on applied skills. If you need a specific certification, tell us what you're working toward and we'll let you know whether we can help or point you in the right direction.

How does an engagement usually start?

With a conversation about your goals, followed by a short discovery or assessment. From there we propose a focused plan, whether that's a workshop, a coaching engagement, or a staged transformation roadmap.

Do you work with non-software teams?

Yes. Agile practices can help marketing, operations, HR, finance, and other knowledge-work teams. Our Agile Beyond Software workshop is designed for them.

How do you measure success?

We agree on a few simple signals with you up front, such as time from idea to customer, delivery predictability, quality, customer feedback, and team health, and review them regularly.

How do I get in touch?

Use the contact form or email info [at] agileand [dot] com.

Let's talk about where your teams want to go

Tell us what you're working on and what's getting in the way. We'll suggest a practical next step, whether that's a workshop, coaching, or a short assessment.
