Services

Coaching and advisory that meets you where you are

From a single team that wants to work better to an organization rethinking how it plans and delivers, we offer hands-on support that builds lasting capability.

Discuss your goals See training
Team coaching

Agile team coaching

We work alongside your teams during real planning, delivery, and review cycles, helping them adopt practices that fit and drop the ones that don't.

  • Observe how the team works today and agree on focus areas
  • Facilitate and model effective planning, reviews, and retrospectives
  • Coach Scrum Masters and team leads so the capability stays in-house
  • Simple, visible signals of improvement agreed with the team
Leadership coaching

Leadership & executive coaching

Leaders shape the system teams work in. We help leaders set clear direction, make trade-offs visible, and support teams without micromanaging.

  • One-to-one and leadership-team coaching
  • Turning strategy into clear, testable goals
  • Decision-making, prioritization, and funding conversations
  • Leading through change with transparency
Transformation advisory

Agile transformation advisory

For organizations changing how work is planned, funded, and delivered across many teams. We help you design a staged roadmap that favors early, visible progress over a big-bang rollout.

  • Current-state discovery and a clear case for change
  • A staged roadmap with small, reversible steps
  • Operating-model and team-structure options
  • Regular checkpoints to adjust based on what you learn
Assessment

Agile assessment

A structured, collaborative look at how your teams and leadership work today, producing a short list of practical recommendations rather than a long report.

  • Interviews, observation, and a lightweight team survey
  • Strengths and gaps across practices, flow, and leadership
  • Prioritized recommendations with suggested next steps
  • A readout session with your leadership team
Product

Product ownership & product management coaching

Help product owners and managers focus on outcomes, build backlogs that tell a clear story, and have better conversations with stakeholders and customers.

  • Outcome-based goals and roadmaps
  • Backlog refinement, slicing, and prioritization
  • Customer discovery and feedback loops
  • Stakeholder alignment and expectation setting
Flow & delivery

Flow & delivery improvement

Make work visible, limit work in progress, and reduce the waiting that slows delivery, using Kanban principles that work alongside whatever process you use today.

  • Visualize end-to-end workflow, including hand-offs
  • Work-in-progress limits and explicit policies
  • Flow measures teams can use to improve themselves
  • Remove recurring blockers and dependencies
Ways to engage

Choose the level of support that fits

Every engagement is scoped to your goals. These are common starting points.

Focused workshop

A short, targeted session to tackle a specific challenge, such as a stalled retrospective habit, unclear priorities, or a new team kickoff.

Coaching engagement

Ongoing coaching for one or more teams or leaders over several cycles, with regular check-ins on agreed goals.

Transformation partnership

Longer-term support across multiple teams and leadership, combining assessment, roadmap, training, and coaching.

The difference

Framework-first rollout vs. the Agile& approach

TopicFramework-first rolloutAgile& approach
Starting pointPick a framework, then apply it everywhereStart from your goals and current constraints
Pace of changeLarge, all-at-once launchSmall, staged steps you can adjust
Measuring progressAdoption of ceremonies and rolesChanges in delivery, quality, and customer feedback
TrainingClassroom sessions, then on your ownTraining paired with coaching on real work
End stateOngoing reliance on outside helpInternal capability to keep improving
